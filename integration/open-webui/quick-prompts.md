# Optional conversational shortcuts

These are text, not registered Open WebUI commands. Copy only what you want to use.

Capture: “Keep this as a rough note. Preserve my words; suggest structure later. If you cannot actually save it, give me a capture-ready record and say it is not saved.”

Compare: “Help me compare these two traces. Keep observations separate from possible explanations, and retain a useful next intervention.”

Direct help: “Just explain this directly. Mark the explanation as supplied and keep my original question available.”

Resume: “Carry forward my current question, recent decisions and reasons, exact evidence revisions, open exceptions, and what is missing. Do not fill gaps from a stale summary.”

Codex preview: “Propose an edition from only these source revisions. Keep failure, contradiction and uncertainty. Separate quotations from your synthesis. Do not overwrite or publish the source record.”
